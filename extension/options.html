Redaction Labels

These mappings control how labels emitted by the model are rewritten. They do not change what the model detects.

Custom Rules

Optional deterministic rules for patterns the built-in model may not know, such as KTP/NIK, NPWP, customer IDs, account numbers, or internal IDs.
